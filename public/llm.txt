# KCET Cutoff

This site helps students estimate likely KCET engineering college options using rank, category, and cutoff trends for Karnataka colleges.

## Website
- https://kcetcutoff.in/

## Purpose
- KCET college predictor for Karnataka engineering admissions
- Rank-based college and branch prediction
- Comparison and analytics across college options
- Help content for students and parents

## Notes
- This tool provides estimates based on cutoff patterns and available data.
- Final seat allotment and admissions decisions are governed by official KEA counseling and verification processes.
